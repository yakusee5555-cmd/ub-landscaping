# Father and Son Landscaping website

## Build

- Create a shared sticky header, mobile navigation, quote buttons, and dark-green footer.
- Build distinct Home, Services, Portfolio, About, and Contact pages with unique search and social metadata.
- Blend the references into one earthy, editorial design: large outdoor imagery, disciplined service/project grids, off-white surfaces, modern sans-serif type, and grounded green accents.
- Use the supplied landscaping photos and video as optimized hosted assets.

## Key experiences

- Home: image-led introduction, service preview, trust signals, reviews, and repeated quote invitation.
- Services: photographed service grid covering lawn care, garden design, hardscaping, hedge care, maintenance, and seasonal cleanup.
- Portfolio: project gallery plus an accessible draggable before/after comparison.
- About: family-business story, experience indicators, and field-work imagery.
- Contact: validated quote form with clear success state, temporary contact placeholders, and a map area pending the real address.

## Technical details

- Keep the app within the existing TanStack Start and Tailwind setup.
- Use semantic design tokens, responsive grids, optimized media loading, accessible controls, and reduced-motion support.
- Verify navigation, mobile layout, the comparison slider, quote form behavior, and preview health.
